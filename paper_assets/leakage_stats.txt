Leakage audit (audit_leakage.py; same check as the last step of build_dataset.py)
Rule: a val image (vs all train) or test image (vs all train + val) is flagged if, for one of its
variants none/hflip/vflip/rot180, both the whole-image pHash and the largest-box-crop pHash are within
Hamming distance 6 of the same reference image. Flagged images are excluded from
val_clean.txt / test_clean.txt; nothing is moved or deleted.
Classes are after the merge rule (< 50 training images -> other_aircraft: Su-30MKI, Jaguar, J-16, J-11, KJ-500, Mirage-III-V, Saab-2000-Erieye).

== Clean lists (R1-R3; label-only, model-independent) ==
Labels are the original exported labels (no corrections applied). An image is in val_clean.txt /
test_clean.txt unless
  R1 leakage:        the flip/rotation leakage check above flags it;
  R2 overlapping GT: two of its GT boxes overlap at IoU >= 0.5;
  R3 stacked GT:     two of its GT boxes share one x-range, |dx0| <= 0.002 and |dx1| <= 0.002
                     (normalised image width; build_dataset.shared_x_groups).
No model predictions and no manual decisions are used. 'removed' = by that rule and not an earlier one.
